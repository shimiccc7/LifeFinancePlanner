# Product Roadmap

## Product principle
Make the next financial action obvious, measurable, and sustainable.

## v0.1 — Baseline
Monthly budget inputs, suggested allocation, travel sinking fund, ESPP ladder, XP missions, localStorage persistence.

## v0.2 — Annual planning
12-month cash-flow calendar; irregular annual expenses; bonus / RSU / ESPP events; ESPP 8%, 10%, 12%, 15% scenario comparison; year-end cash projection.

## v0.3 — Goal engine
Emergency fund runway; travel goals; debt reduction; education goals; diversified investment target; goal progress and streaks.

## v1.0 — Optional sync
Authentication; encrypted user data; multi-device sync; export/import.

## Guardrails
- Never hard-code private user financial data in Git.
- Gamification rewards plan adherence, not deprivation or excessive investing.
- RSU/ESPP modules remain employer-agnostic.
